How the pickup works

  1. You book here. A job card is created.
  2. On Saturday or Sunday our partner arrives in your chosen time slot.
  3. A 10-second video of your watch is recorded in front of you, showing any existing scratches or dents. This protects you and us.
  4. The shop's technician inspects it during the week and sends you a cost estimate.
  5. You approve or decline on the tracking page. Decline and we return it unrepaired.
  6. After repair, we deliver it back on the next weekend and you pay at the door.
